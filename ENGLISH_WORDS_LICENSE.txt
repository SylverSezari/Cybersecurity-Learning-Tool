The English entries in english_words.txt are derived from:
https://github.com/first20hours/google-10000-english/blob/master/google-10000-english-no-swears.txt

Data files are derived from the *Google Web Trillion Word Corpus*, as described by Thorsten Brants and Alex Franz (http://googleresearch.blogspot.com/2006/08/all-our-n-gram-are-belong-to-you.html), and distributed by the Linguistic Data Consortium (http://www.ldc.upenn.edu/Catalog/CatalogEntry.jsp?catalogId=LDC2006T13). Subsets of this corpus are distributed by Peter Norvig (http://norvig.com/ngrams/). Corpus editing and cleanup by Josh Kaufman.
